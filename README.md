# N-RUN!

**Pick your hero · Grab your weapon · Build your worlds**

A pixel-art auto-runner game created by **Naiyan Shah, 8 years old** · Est. 2026

Play it here: https://milan470.github.io/n-run/

- 7 playable characters, each with their own special power
- 10 weapons — any weapon works with any hero
- Cartridge chasers, diagonal bouncers and 4-way laser turrets to defeat
- Day / sunset / night / space background modes
- A full level editor — build courses and share them with friends via course codes
